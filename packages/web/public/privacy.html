Privacy Policy

Updated November 16, 2026

Who we are

SummonGhost, Inc. ("we") publishes summonghost.com, a web app that lets you talk to Ghost, the open-source app on your own computers.

What we collect

Nothing. summonghost.com has no server of its own, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. Cloudflare serves its files and nothing else.

Where your data goes

Your browser talks straight to your own machines over your Tailscale network. Your messages, photos and conversations travel only between this browser and those machines, and are kept on those machines by Ghost. They never pass through us.

Tailscale tells each machine who is calling. That happens inside your own tailnet, under Tailscale's terms and policy.

What stays in your browser

The list of machines you add, and the ghosts last seen on each, are kept in this browser's storage so the app remembers them. They stay on this device; remove a machine in the app, or clear the site's data, to delete them. The app also keeps a copy of its own files so it opens offline.

Cloudflare

Like any web host, Cloudflare handles the requests that load the app's files, including your IP address, and may keep short-lived logs to run and protect its network under its own privacy policy. We do not use those logs to track anyone.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if this ever changes. The date above shows the latest version.
