SummonGhost

Your ghost on each machine, from anywhere on your tailnet

SummonGhost talks straight to Ghost on your own computers over Tailscale. It has no server and no account, and nothing you do here goes anywhere but your machines.

  1. On a computer with Ghost, run tailscale serve --bg --https=443 http://127.0.0.1:7717.
  2. Open the link that ghost status then prints, or type the machine's name below.
  3. The other computers you own on the tailnet are found from there.

This device needs Tailscale, signed in as you.
